Jarvis Assistant

Jarvis Assistant (JJ) is a personal AI assistant for Johnny Wilson. It helps with everyday tasks by connecting to Google services the owner authorizes — including Calendar, Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Tasks, Fitness, Sheets, Photos, and YouTube (read-only where applicable) — so it can schedule, summarize, and act on the owner’s behalf on their own devices.

This is a personal project, not a public multi-tenant product. Access is limited to the owner’s Google account.
